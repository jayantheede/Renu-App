Grower portal prototype v2

Clickable mock-up of the ReNu-Biome customer portal, updated Oct 21, 2026 to the single requirements document: Part A, the Phase 1 fixes for the February 2027 release, and Part B, Phase 2. One fictional pilot customer: 1,400 acres of almonds on two ranches. All names and numbers are sample data.

Try: sign in as the customer, open Home → Home Ranch → a program, send lab results from a block, open a delivery that is out for delivery. Then More → Company admin: mark today's delivery delivered and enter its invoice, create an order from a phone call, review the soil sample and send a recommendation.

Items marked PHASE 2 are Part B; the switch under More → Prototype v2 hides them to show Part A, the February 2027 release, alone.
