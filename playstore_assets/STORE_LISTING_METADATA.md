### Full Description (max 4,000 characters)
```
ReNu-Biome is the official operations, agronomy, and bulk liquid replenishment platform built for commercial growers, orchards, vineyards, and agricultural operations.

Seamlessly connecting remote farm infrastructure to real-time wireless telematics, ReNu-Biome prevents costly biological soil inoculant runouts and simplifies commercial fertilizer logistics across all your ranch blocks.

KEY CAPABILITIES:

1. REAL-TIME TANK TELEMETRY
• Live cellular sensor monitoring of bulk on-farm storage tanks (gallons, inches, and percentage fill).
• Proactive low-level threshold alerts (30%, 15%, critical runout warnings).
• Battery health and signal telemetry tracking via Otodata Nee-Vo telematics.

2. CROP NUTRITION PROGRAMS
• Detailed biological application schedules for Almonds, Pistachios, Citrus, Grapes, and specialty crops.
• Block-level rate tracking (gallons/acre), application stages (Pre-Bloom, Bloom, Post-Harvest), and target nutrient delivery.
• Agronomist recommendations and customized foliar and fertigation schedules.

3. DISPATCH & LIVE DELIVERY TRACKING
• Real-time visibility into commercial tanker truck deliveries dispatched directly to remote ranch blocks.
• Turn-by-turn GPS navigation for delivery drivers to exact tank manifolds lacking street addresses.
• Driver proof-of-delivery confirmation, metered gallons, and automatic ledger updates.

4. COMMERCIAL INVOICES & STATEMENTS
• Transparent invoice tracking, downloadable PDF statements, and year-end nitrogen/yield reports.
• Convenient B2B ACH and invoice settlement for commercial agricultural goods.

5. ENTERPRISE AGRONOMY COLLABORATION
• Dedicated messaging with assigned regional agronomists and operations managers.
• Soil and lab sample analysis tracking, historical application logs, and ranch management.

6. CDFA & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
• Biological soil inoculants and organic crop nutrition compliant with California Department of Food and Agriculture (CDFA) Organic Input Material (OIM) standards and AAPFCO guidelines.

ACCOUNT SECURITY & DATA PRIVACY:
ReNu-Biome features enterprise-grade two-factor authentication (MFA/OTP), complete encryption in transit, and transparent in-app account deletion controls under your profile settings.
```
