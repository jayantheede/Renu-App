ReNu Biome Mobile Application Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

ReNu-Biome Inc. ("ReNu Biome", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and security of our commercial growers, agricultural partners, and users. This Privacy Policy specifically governs the collection, use, and protection of information within the ReNu Biome Mobile Application for iOS and Android, as well as the accompanying ReNu Biome Grower Portal services (collectively, the "Application").

This policy applies solely to the ReNu Biome Mobile Application and related agricultural logistics tools, and is distinct from policies governing consumer e-commerce storefronts.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver agricultural biological products, monitor on-farm storage telemetry, and provide seamless delivery logistics:

  • Account & Registration Details: User name, email address, password hash, phone number, and affiliated commercial farm or ranch legal business name.
  • Agricultural & Operational Data: Field ranch names, biological storage tank capacities, current liquid volume levels, threshold alerts, biological program stages (e.g., Almond Nutrition Programs), and crop treatment schedules.
  • Delivery & Logistics Data: Scheduled and completed delivery records, destination coordinates, dispatch timeframes, and confirmation signatures.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Device model, operating system version, and anonymous crash telemetry strictly utilized to diagnose stability issues and optimize application performance.

Note on Tracking: The ReNu Biome Application does NOT track users across third-party websites or apps for advertising purposes. We do NOT use third-party advertising SDKs, and we never sell user data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data strictly to fulfill agricultural service agreements and provide core app features:

  • Facilitating scheduled bulk tanker deliveries of biological soil inoculants.
  • Displaying real-time tank level monitoring to prevent runouts during critical crop fertilization windows.
  • Generating and displaying commercial delivery-linked invoices and statements.
  • Sending essential transactional security notices, such as account verification and delivery completion alerts.
  • Maintaining and troubleshooting application stability.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal or agricultural information. Data is shared only with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate the service:

  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Secure database and server hosting (e.g., Render, MongoDB) operating in enterprise US data centers with encryption at rest and in transit.
  • Transactional Messaging Providers: Enterprise mail transmission services used solely to deliver transactional security notices and account lifecycle confirmations.
  • Mapping & Navigation Services: Apple Maps / Google Maps for displaying delivery destination markers and field coordinates.

5. Account Deletion & Data Rights (Guideline 5.1.1v)

We respect your right to control your personal and farm data. In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v), account deletion is available directly within the mobile application without requiring an email or phone call.

How to Delete Your Account In-App:

  • Open the ReNu Biome app and sign in.
  • Navigate to More → Account Session → tap "Delete Account" (or go to Profile → tap "Delete Account & Data").
  • Review the destructive confirmation dialog and tap "Permanently Delete".

Upon confirmation, the server immediately executes permanent deletion of your user account credentials, active sessions, and personal profile information from our active databases, and logs out the application. You may also contact us at support@renu-biome.com to request account or data removal.

6. Data Security & Encryption

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. All network communication between the mobile app and backend servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Sensitive credentials and tokens are hashed and stored using secure device storage (Keychain on iOS).

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is a commercial B2B agricultural logistics platform designed for commercial growers and agricultural operators. It is not intended for or marketed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal updates. Any revisions will be posted directly within the Application and at this URL with an updated effective date.
