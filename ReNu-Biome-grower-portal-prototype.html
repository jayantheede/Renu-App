Grower portal prototype

Clickable mock-up of the grower side of the ReNu-Biome customer portal. Growers, ranches, prices and results are sample data.

Try: switch entity on Home, accept the recommendation in Ranches → N-4, pay two invoices, send a message, then open More → Company admin as Owner to confirm the order you requested, or as CEO and CTO for the business and systems views.

On a phone this fills the screen. Nothing is saved between visits except your language choice.
